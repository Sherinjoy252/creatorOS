Information We Collect Depending on the features a user authorizes and uses, CreatorOS may process the following information: - **LinkedIn account information:** Name, email address, and LinkedIn member identifier obtained through LinkedIn sign-in. - **Content information:** Topics, writing samples, generated drafts, brand voice profiles, hashtags, and content approved by users. - **LinkedIn post information:** Post identifiers, post text, publication dates, and engagement metrics, but only when the relevant LinkedIn API access and user authorization permit retrieval. - **Usage and technical information:** Application activity, error information, and technical data needed to operate and improve the service. CreatorOS does not intentionally request a user's LinkedIn password. ## 3. How We Use Information CreatorOS may use the information described above to: - Authenticate users and maintain their application sessions. - Analyse writing samples and generate personalized brand voice profiles. - Generate, review, and refine professional content. - Publish content to LinkedIn when the user authorizes the relevant action. - Analyse available post performance metrics and improve future content recommendations. - Maintain application security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability. ## 4. AI Processing and Third-Party Services CreatorOS uses Mesh API to route AI and language-model requests, as required by the project's architecture. Content submitted for AI processing may be transmitted to Mesh API and the relevant model provider to fulfil the requested operation. Such processing is subject to the applicable providers' terms, privacy policies, and data-handling practices. CreatorOS also uses LinkedIn's authentication and other APIs when the necessary permissions are available. Information obtained from LinkedIn is handled subject to applicable LinkedIn terms and developer policies. CreatorOS does not claim that third-party providers retain no data. Users should consult the applicable providers' policies for information about their processing and retention practices. ## 5. Data Storage and Security CreatorOS uses application storage to maintain user records and, as applicable, generated content, brand voice profiles, and post performance information. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards will be used to protect stored information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion CreatorOS retains information only for as long as necessary to provide its features, maintain application security, meet applicable obligations, or resolve disputes. CreatorOS will implement appropriate deletion and account-disconnection procedures as the application develops. ## 7.
